# The Long Run: script (v2)

One line runs through the whole film. A stranger says it to Mark at his first parkrun in 2016, and it comes back whenever he forgets it:

> "If you can't talk, you're going too fast."

Under the surface the film follows the hero's journey: ordinary world, call, mentor, refusal, threshold, allies and enemies, the ordeal, the reward, the road back, resurrection, and the return. None of that is ever named on screen. Every date, time and quote comes from Mark's Strava log and parkrun results. The two marathons' pacing follows his watch data.

Format: 480×270 pixel art, letterboxed to 2.35:1, subtitles in the lower bar, about 6¼ minutes.

## Cold open
- A ridge at dawn. A lone runner in silhouette crosses the skyline. Wind. A whisper: *"If you can't talk…"* Cut to black.
- Title: **THE LONG RUN**.

## I. The false start
- **Norwich, May 2016.** A student room at night. His phone buzzes: *"parkrun tomorrow? 9am"* … *"what's parkrun"* … *"5k. free. every saturday"* … *"…fine."* The alarm goes off at 7:45. He groans.
- **Eaton Park.** A crowd by the bandstand, *3, 2, 1*. He bolts off far too fast, then fades. An older runner draws level, relaxed: *"First time?"* Gasping: *"…yeah."*
- In close-up the stranger says: *"If you can't talk, you're going too fast."* Mark eases off. He finishes in **26:21**, 308th. The stranger waves and is gone into the crowd.
- The trainers go under the bed. Calendar pages fall away: 2017, 2018. **St Albans, Sept 2019**: five parkruns and a 24:56 PB, then the cupboard door shuts again.
- **March 2020.** Empty streets. *parkrun: all events cancelled.*

## II. Across the threshold
- **London, 14 May 2020.** A hand pulls the dusty trainers out. The front door opens onto bright light. The first run on the log: 5.07 km at 7:29/km.
- *"Ouch my shins."*
- July: he's chatting to a stranger in the park and forgets to pause his watch (*"Not-on-the-pace run"*). For a second the old man's smile flickers in sepia.
- By August he's running 5:17/km.

## III. Allies and enemies
- **Finsbury Park, Aug 2021.** parkrun is back: hi-vis volunteers, dogs, a crowd. (*"Ed Miliband @ parkrun."*)
- The seasons turn, autumn leaves, frost, blossom, summer, and his PB falls from 25:25 to 20:15.
- Slow Sunday runs with Megan. They talk the whole way.
- **May 2022: runner's knee.** Fifteen days to the Hackney Half. An ice pack on the sofa, the calendar counting down. *"Parkrun – testing the knee."*
- **Hackney Half, 22 May 2022.** It's touch and go. The knee holds: **1:46:30**, *"the knee held out."* Far up the road bobs a pacer's balloon marked **1:30**.
- The Great North Run (1:38:12), the Royal Parks Half, Tuesday nights on the track (*"missed the last rep, rushed off to pub quiz"*), sub-20, sub-40.
- **Hackney Half 2023.** The balloon is closer, but still ahead: **1:35:30**, *"an ambitious attempt at 1:30 but happy with 1:35."*

## IV. The ordeal
- **Eight weeks to Richmond.** Long runs at dawn: 26, 28, 30, 32 km. *"I have become death, destroyer of long runs."* The forecast for race day: 30°C.
- **Richmond Marathon, 10 Sept 2023.** Heat haze. By 22 km his pace fades from 5:00 to 6:30 per km. The water stations are empty and runners are down on the kerb.
- In close-up, sweat-soaked, the old man's words come back. He slows to a pace he can survive.
- A megaphone: *"The race has been stopped."* The barriers close and he gets in to cross the line: **3:55:11**. He lies flat on the grass, the medal on his chest, the sky white with heat.

## V. The reward
- **Christmas Day 2023, Milford Waterfront.** His 100th parkrun, in a Santa hat, 2nd behind an ironman.
- 2024: the shingles parkrun, a 39:50 10k, and at last sub-19 at Finsbury.
- **Hackney Half, 19 May 2024.** The 1:30 balloon is ahead. He reels it in and passes it. Slow motion, and the crowd noise drops away: **1:29:01**.
- The Big Half (1:28:45), then the **Robin Hood Half, 29 Sept 2024: 1:24:17.** He is flying.

## VI. The road back
- **Ten weeks to Valencia.** The training calendar fills faster and faster. The old man's words flicker, and he runs straight through them.
- **15 Oct 2024.** *Crack.* Sore ankles. Then rain on the window, a foot up on the sofa: a posterior tibial stress injury from overtraining. *"Valencia marathon dream over."* Weights: *"absolute snooze fest."*
- **Valencia, 1 Dec 2024.** Blue sky and the City of Arts and Sciences. He cheers the lads home, then there's paella and a cold drink. A toast: *"We will come back stronger."*

## VII. Resurrection
- **Dec 2024.** A dark morning run with David, chatting all the way: *"One for the comeback montage."* The comeback parkrun: *"full send, then big regrets."*
- **1 Mar 2025.** His 95th and last Finsbury parkrun. A van heads north, and St Albans appears at golden hour.
- 2025: 18:28 at Rickmansworth, **1:26:28** at Hackney, 1:26:21 on the St Albans hills, 18:19 for 5k.
- **Manchester Marathon, 19 Apr 2026.** Eighteen weeks ticked off. He holds 4:05–4:15/km, Achilles grumbling from the start, until **28 km, where the wall rises**. He can't talk. He keeps going: **3:20:03**, 35 minutes faster than Richmond. *"The wall won. But now I know."*
- Two days later: *Walk-to-the-end-of-my-street-with-crutches 2026: 5:00, new PB.*

## VIII. The return
- Tokyo, *"hot hot hot"*. A puppy.
- **Coaching Megan** (*"the road to Megan's sub-2 half starts now"*). She's pushing too hard. He runs beside her and passes on the line: *"If you can't talk, you're going too fast."* She laughs.
- The Sixer with the lads. The St Albans Claw.
- **Today, 26 Sept 2026, St Albans parkrun: 18:43**, a course PB. *"Where did that come from."*
- The dawn ridge again. This time it's a group running along it, Mark, Megan, the lads and the puppy, talking all the way. The whisper finishes: *"…you're going too fast."*
- **THE LONG RUN.** 1,230 runs · 9,199 km · 186 parkruns · one piece of advice.
- Credits roll beside outtakes from the log. Last card: *See you Saturday. 9am.*
