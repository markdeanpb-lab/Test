# The Long Run

A short pixel-art film about Mark Dean's running, written entirely in code: a 480×270 software framebuffer, a hand-drawn bitmap font, jointed runner puppets, close-up portraits and an original score. It runs about 6¼ minutes in 105 shots, from a 7:45 alarm in a Norwich student room (May 2016) to an 18:43 course PB at St Albans on 26 Sep 2026.

One line holds it together. A stranger in Eaton Park tells a gasping first-timer "if you can't talk, you're going too fast". The line comes back when Mark forgets it, and at the end he is the one saying it.

## Watch it

- `output/the-long-run.mp4`: the rendered film (1920×1080, 30 fps, with sound).
- `output/the-long-run-720p.mp4`: a smaller copy for sharing.
- `output/the-long-run.html`: the same film as one self-contained page with play/pause, scrubbing, chapters and the live score. Open it in any browser.
- `index.html`: the development version of that page (loads the files in `src/` and `data/`).

## What's in it

Every date, time, place and quote comes from Mark's own records, or from what he told us:

- **Strava**: 1,230 runs (9,199 km) logged between 14 May 2020 and 26 Sep 2026. Activity titles and descriptions appear on screen as "Mark's log" captions ("Ouch my shins", "I have become death, destroyer of long runs", "The Wall Won… But now I know"). The Richmond and Manchester marathon scenes show pace and heart rate from the watch streams (`data/race-streams.json`).
- **parkrun**: all 186 results from [parkrunner 2425906](https://www.parkrun.org.uk/parkrunner/2425906/): 19 events, 40 course PBs, 5 podiums, PB 18:28.
- **From Mark**: the false start at university, runner's knee before the first Hackney Half, the years chasing 1:30 at Hackney, Richmond in the heatwave (water ran out and the race was stopped as he got through), and the posterior tibial stress injury that ended the Valencia build, followed by a trip to cheer his friends and eat paella anyway.

Chapters: Dawn · Norwich, 2016 · The lost years · Lockdown · Finsbury Park · The knee · Chasing 1:30 · Richmond · The golden year · Valencia · The comeback · Manchester · Coach · Today · Credits. `STORY.md` has the full script.

## Files

| Path | What it does |
|---|---|
| `data/strava-runs.txt` | Every Strava run: date and distance (rides, swims, walks and gym sessions left out, one duplicate recording removed). |
| `data/parkrun-results.json` | Every parkrun result, parsed from the parkrun profile page. |
| `data/race-streams.json` | Distance, time and heart-rate streams for the Richmond (2023) and Manchester (2026) marathons. |
| `data/career.js` | Generated by `tools/build-data.mjs`: totals, per-year kilometres, PB progressions and the race streams. |
| `src/engine.js` | Framebuffer, palette, dithering, drawing primitives, the 5×7 proportional font, and film-style grading (colour grade, sepia, heat haze, vignette). |
| `src/art.js` | The cast: jointed runner puppets (run, sprint, limp, hobble, crutches, lift, lie…) with expressions, close-up portraits, and props (phone, alarm clock, watch, calendar, balloon, puppy, the Wall, paella). |
| `src/world.js` | Skies, parallax scenery, parks, streets, the London and St Albans skylines, the Tyne Bridge, Valencia, rooms, crowds and weather. |
| `src/film.js` | Film grammar: letterbox, subtitles, location titles, log captions, stat cards, fades and the shot timeline. |
| `src/story.js` | The 105 shots, their music and ambience cues, and the chapter list. |
| `src/audio.js` | The score: about 20 cues and the leitmotif, played through chip pulse, triangle and noise voices plus bell, pad and nylon-guitar voices with a shared reverb, and ambience beds (wind, birds, crowd, rain, heartbeat, clock). The same event list plays live and renders offline for the video. |
| `src/player.js` | The page player, plus the `?render` mode that the video exporter drives. |
| `tools/build-page.mjs` | Inlines everything into `output/the-long-run.html`. |
| `tools/render-video.mjs` | Steps the film frame by frame in headless Chromium, renders the score with an `OfflineAudioContext`, and encodes both with ffmpeg. |

## Rebuild

```bash
node tools/build-data.mjs                    # data/career.js from the data files
node tools/build-page.mjs                    # output/the-long-run.html
FFMPEG=/path/to/ffmpeg node tools/render-video.mjs                                    # output/the-long-run.mp4
FFMPEG=/path/to/ffmpeg node tools/render-video.mjs --sheet 5,60,120 --out stills.png  # stills at those seconds
FFMPEG=/path/to/ffmpeg node tools/render-video.mjs --sheet shots:0-11 --out shots.png # one still per shot
```

The video exporter needs Playwright with Chromium and an ffmpeg built with libx264 and AAC (`pip install imageio-ffmpeg` ships one; `python3 -c "import imageio_ffmpeg; print(imageio_ffmpeg.get_ffmpeg_exe())"` prints its path).

Nothing here is wired into the St Albans Racing game in the rest of the repository; the folder stands alone.
