# RUNNING GEAR SOLID: REMASTERED — game design

The film is a highlights reel of someone **playing** a tactical-espionage game whose campaign is
a real running career. The rules for every chapter:

- **Cutscenes** carry the story: in-engine, letterboxed, subtitled, with codec calls.
- **Gameplay** is shown with the game's HUD on screen: LIFE and STAMINA, the equipped ITEM and
  EQUIP, the boss's health bar, controller prompts, damage numbers, ITEM GET, MISSION COMPLETE or
  FAILED, results screens with a code name, CONTINUE? screens.
- **Every boss is its own level**: a unique arena, its own genre, and a mechanic that the player
  is visibly trying to beat. The real race data drives the fight (splits are the damage, pace is the
  weapon, HR is the heat), so the numbers still come from Strava.
- **Running footage on the real courses is kept short.** It's the "level" between cutscenes, never
  the whole show.
- **Text is readable on a phone**: nothing below 32 px at 1080p, and codec lines sit under the
  speaker's portrait.

## Framing

- **Opening**: the Manchester start line (flash-forward), then "A line.", the gun, and "SIX YEARS
  EARLIER" as a save file loading.
- **Between bosses**: short race highlights with the HUD, codec calls, briefings, and
  CONTINUE?/near-miss screens (20:37, 20:34, 20:15 as RETRY cards).
- **Ending**: the mission list (SUB 3 and SUB 18 INCOMPLETE), SAVE GAME?, "NEW MISSION DETECTED",
  and the runner moves again.
