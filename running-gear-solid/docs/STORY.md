# RUNNING GEAR SOLID: REMASTERED: story bible

## The idea that holds it together

**Every line you draw grows a guardian.** A target (sub 20, sub 1:30, sub 3) is a line, and once
STRIDE draws one, something comes to hold it. TEMPO says it the first time a target goes on the
board ("Once you draw a line, something comes to hold it"). The Wall is the last guardian and the
biggest line. The prologue ("A line.") and chapter 7 ("THE LINE") pay it off.

Two guardians aren't lines but faults in the runner: **THE HARE** (impatience: it waits at start
lines) and **HAIRLINE** (the body). Neither can be beaten by trying harder. That's the point.

## The support team (codec)

- **MAJOR TEMPO**: coach and commander. Dry. He knows the guardians by name.
- **DR. LACTATE**: medic. Knee, shingles, ankles, heat.
- **GEARBOX**: kit and tech (the Otacon). Shoes, and the PACE GOGGLES.

## Every boss gets the same arc

**omen, codec (what is it?), escalation, plan (how to beat it = the mechanic), fight, aftermath.**
Dates on screen are real activities. The dialogue is fiction, but it never states a fact the data
doesn't back.
