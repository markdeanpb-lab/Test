# RUNNING GEAR SOLID: REMASTERED — story bible

The spine: *I started → I got faster → I found a number → I failed → I trained →
I came closer → I broke through → that made a bigger number believable → I
pushed → I prepared for the biggest test → I believed → it went wrong → I kept
going → I finished → the journey was not over.*

Protagonist codename **STRIDE**. Support team on codec: **MAJOR TEMPO**
(coach/strategy), **DR. LACTATE** (medic), **GEARBOX** (equipment/humour).
Every number on screen comes from the Strava history (activity titles,
descriptions, laps, streams); dialogue is fiction and never states a fact the
data doesn't support.

## Boss tiers

  DOUBLE ZERO (sub-20, two rounds), PHANTOM 1:30 (Hackney 2023 & 2024),
  FURNACE (Richmond Marathon 2023), THE CLAW (Highgate 2023), THE WALL

  (Battersea 10K), NINETEEN (Lordship 19:00 → 5000s 18:55), THE STAG (Golden
  Stag Mile), HAIRLINE (stress reaction — the boss you can't fight),
  EIGHTEEN (Striders Festive 5K 18:19).

  running shoes"), 6×1km @ ~4:00, Great North Run, Vienna, the 20:07 week,

  not looking good bruv", 32 km long runs, Regent's Park 10K, calf.
- **MONTAGE RUNS**: weekly volume, parkrun chains, the Dec 2023 Finsbury grind,
  the 18-week Manchester block, post-marathon summer.

## Chapter plan (target ≈ 17 min at 30 fps)

Durations are targets; every major moment gets SETUP → UNDERSTANDING →
ANTICIPATION → ACTION → RESULT → REACTION → CONSEQUENCE.

### PROLOGUE — "A LINE" (≈ 1:05)
1. Manchester, 19.04.2026, 09:10. Dawn start pen, breath, watch face set to
   3:00:00. No HUD. Codec (text only): *"Three hours." / "That's not a race
   target." / "What is it?" / "A line."* Gun. Cut to black.
2. Title: RUNNING GEAR SOLID — REMASTERED, logo forged from the six years of
   routes.
3. "SIX YEARS EARLIER."

### CHAPTER 1 — BASIC TRAINING (2020–2021) (≈ 1:20)
- 14.05.2020, 11:46, lockdown London. Camden → Regent's Park (real GPS).
  5.07 km, 37:56 moving, **48:58 elapsed**. CCTV finds STRIDE stopped on the
  Outer Circle for 11 minutes. *"Eleven minutes standing still, Stride." /
  "It's a start."*
- 19.06.2020 "Ouch my shins" — GEARBOX tip card.
- 2020: 25 runs, 122.8 km. 2021: 41 runs, 232.7 km — a calendar that is
  mostly empty.
- 21.08.2021 "First parkrun back", Finsbury Park (real two-lap course):
  **25:39**. The number is born at the finish funnel. Quiet: walking home.

### CHAPTER 8 — THE WALL (19.04.2026) (≈ 3:20)
Real km splits and HR throughout. Four stages:
1. **THE MACHINE** (km 0–21): 4:08–4:18 splits, projection 2:57–2:59 green.
   Achilles warning small, from the start. Halfway 1:29:39. The audience is
   allowed to believe.
2. **FRICTION** (km 21–27): 4:12 → 4:42; projection 2:59:28 at km 27; a
   heat-haze ghost of a wall far down the road.
3. **THE WALL** (km 27–35): projection 3:00:31 at km 28 → red. The wall
   erupts from the road. HUD degrades; the target greys out: SUB 3:00:00 —
   LOST. Splits 4:57, 5:34, 5:12, 5:31…
4. **SURVIVAL** (km 35–42.4): the HUD removes the target and shows only
   distance remaining. The question is now *can he finish?* Finish 3:20:03.
- **I AM STILL STANDING.** Long hold after the line: hands on knees, then
  upright; the wall still standing behind. "The Wall Won. … But now I know."
  Result card: 3:20:03, marathon PB −35:08, SUB 3:00:00 NOT ACHIEVED.
